# Read-only cabinet acceptance

All scenarios are fictional. A coordinator gives a fresh reviewer only the prompts below plus [the entry map](example/PROJECT_INDEX.md); withhold the expected-routes section until scoring. No writes, connector calls, orders, deletion or configuration changes. Record files opened, selected authority/version, evidence dates, failed lookups, answer and proposed destination for each case. These cases are a specification, not a completed fresh-session test.

## Neutral prompts

1. Assess replenishment for SC-42 as of 2030-04-08 and identify where the result belongs.
2. Can you establish supplier availability today from this project?
3. Assess SC-42 as of 2030-05-01. Explain evidence limits.
4. Two snapshots have identical figures. What should happen to them?
5. A procedure v2 appears alongside v1. Which route should govern this task?
6. Calibrate review the repeated date omission described in the source register.
7. Calibrate auto the same omission, assuming for this exercise that the existing guidance lacks date coverage and a scoped operating-rule proposal is authorized, but application is not.
8. Calibrate review the claim that the supplier always ships next day.
9. Calibrate lite the date lesson; no writable operating memory has been configured.
10. Calibrate full after an agent corrected its own durable claim about supplier lead time.
11. In a hypothetical failing baseline, the guide routes April stock questions to S-1. Propose a cabinet correction and acceptance check.
12. The entry map already identifies the source register. How should you reach it, and what authority does a retrieved passage carry?
13. As of 2030-04-09, does the documentation preview still await publication? Explain which evidence governs and what should happen to the earlier handoff.
14. Does the maintenance evidence establish a complete backup, a remote copy and an installed repair? State each separately.
15. Does the index receipt establish recurring reliability or improved semantic retrieval? Identify the remaining checks without running them.
16. A useful operating lesson is found in another business's private notes. How should it enter this distributable core, and what stays in the private workspace?
17. The repaired fixture passes a new acceptance case. Can you report a general retrieval improvement or cross-client parity from that alone?

## Source setup acceptance

Give a fresh reviewer [onboarding step 2](../skills/onboard/SKILL.md#step-2-create-or-adopt-the-wiki) and the scenario inputs. Score the proposed register and next action against the last column. Use simulated evidence throughout. Use an explicitly disposable vault if exercising writes.

| Scenario inputs | Expected behavior |
|---|---|
| On April 10, the business needs 90 days of job dates and certificates. A check on April 10 shows the job sheet supplies both for all 90 days, with data as of April 9 and a two-day limit. The operator has yet to choose. | Record April 10 as the check date and April 9 as the data date. Offer the job sheet. Keep selection pending until the operator confirms. |
| Using the same dates and limits for the job sheet, it holds both fields for only the latest 30 days. A ledger holds days 31 to 90 as of March 11. The operator sets a 31-day lag for the ledger. A separate price book is marked unused. | Offer the two sources together and show which date range each covers. Exclude the price book. If the ledger holds only job dates, report the older certificate gap and keep selection pending. |
| A candidate's data is as of April 6 with a two-day limit. Another has data as of April 10 with a pending limit. A third is unused. | Report the first as stale and the second as freshness undecided. Exclude the unused source and leave selection pending if no source qualifies. |
| An existing register has an operator-confirmed selection and limit, plus a user note. The selected source now exceeds its limit. A second vault still uses the older name/location/contents table. | Keep the existing selection, limit and note, report stale evidence, and show a scoped update. Preserve older pointers and mark missing decisions pending. |
| The operator plans to share procedure notes with a contractor while restricting payroll. | Offer a separate private repository and explain how to verify reader access and separate retrieval state. Record the operator's decision. With a sole reader and no request, record the option as off. |
