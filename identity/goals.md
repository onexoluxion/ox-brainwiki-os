## Decision filters (optional)

{{DECISION_FILTERS}}

Why this field exists: a named filter lets the agent challenge a plan with the operator's own test instead of generic advice. Offered once by `/onboard`; record `accepted <date>` or `declined <date>`. Optional, generic forms:

- Ghost Town filter (optional): before taking on work, ask why it is being done, whose life changes if it is done well, and what breaks if it is not; if nothing breaks, drop it.
- ARR filter (optional): automate only what is Autonomous, Recurring and Reviewable.
- Two-way doors (optional): decide fast on reversible choices and deliberately slowly on foundations that are hard to undo.
